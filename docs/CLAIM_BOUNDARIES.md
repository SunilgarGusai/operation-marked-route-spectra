# Supported results and boundaries

## Exact statements checked

The four SymPy scripts verify the operation-labelled seven-vertex seed pairs, characteristic-polynomial identities, the polynomial clone-width separation, the matrix-pencil identity, and a template-specific balanced edge-transfer formula. Numerical tests are evidence about the formulas; human mathematical proof review remains essential for journal submission.

## Exploratory experiment

The NAS-Bench-101 and NAS-Bench-201 grouped cross-validation is **development-only exploratory analysis** and was informed by earlier analyses. Cluster-wise bootstrap confidence intervals of the incremental weighted-feature improvements include zero on both benchmarks. The reported results **do not establish predictive superiority**.

## Not represented as replicated

The public checkout does **not** contain official training-label caches, raw benchmark downloads, full model checkpoints, nor every input needed for a full independent 288-model refit. Its numerical-summary checker is not a refit. Neither the official end-to-end NASGraph/DAG-S baseline nor a complete official GRAF experimental pipeline is claimed to be implemented.

## Reserved outcomes

617 NAS-Bench-101 architecture labels and 2,496 NAS-Bench-201 architecture labels were reserved and were not used in the reported development-only results, according to archived records.

## Novelty boundary

Operation-restricted paths, basic graph spectral theory, uniform graph replication, and VELE itself have prior art. Equivalence to other weighted switching mechanisms has not been fully ruled out.
